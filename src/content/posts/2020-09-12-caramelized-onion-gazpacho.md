I love gazpacho for the first recipe of the season. This Caramelized Onion Gazpacho is just the thing to kick off the season. It’s got the classic freshness of gazpacho, but it also has a beautiful caramelized onion that gives it that extra something.

The best thing about this recipe is that it’s easy. I’ve been making gazpacho for years, but it’s always been really simple. You chop up some vegetables and then blend them. That’s it. This Caramelized Onion Gazpacho is just a little more involved, but still just as simple.

Slice some onions and cook them in a pan with some olive oil. Simmer with broth, wine and vinegar. Blend. Stir in tomato juice, ice and more vegetables. Chill.

It’s that simple. It’s still really simple. But it’s also delicious and has that something extra that makes it a little more involved. It will also make a big batch.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cups thinly sliced yellow onions (about 2 large onions)
- 1 1/2 cups vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1/4 cup sherry vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 cups tomato juice
- 1 cup ice cubes
- 1/2 cup diced cucumber
- 1/2 cup each diced red, green and yellow bell pepper
- 1/2 cup diced celery
- 1/2 cup diced zucchini
- 1/4 cup each chopped fresh basil, cilantro and mint

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium-low heat. Add onions and cook, stirring every few minutes, until soft and deep golden brown, 30 to 35 minutes. Add a splash of the broth if they start to stick.
2. Add the vegetable broth, wine, vinegar, salt and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Let cool for 10 minutes, then transfer to a blender and puree until smooth, venting the lid.
4. Pour mixture into a large bowl and stir in tomato juice, ice cubes, cucumber, all three bell peppers, celery, zucchini and herbs.
5. Cover and chill for at least 2 hours, until cold.
