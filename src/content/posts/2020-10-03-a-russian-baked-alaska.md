Every so often I get to thinking about history, and the thing I always come back to is that Alaska used to be Russian. Russia just sold it. Can you imagine? I can’t even part with a casserole dish I borrowed from my sister-in-law in 2014.

So I decided to give Alaska back, at least in dessert form. My grandmother always said a good dessert should feel like a long winter: cold, a little dramatic, and over before you’re ready. She never made a baked Alaska in her life, but I like to think she would have understood.

My husband asked me what makes it Russian, and honestly, I think it’s the attitude. It doesn’t smile. It doesn’t need to.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (480 ml) heavy cream
- 1/2 cup (100 g) sugar
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) water
- 1/4 cup (60 g) unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 large egg whites
- 1/4 cup (30 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (60 g) cake flour
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (240 ml) boiling water
- 1/2 cup (100 g) sugar
- 1/2 cup (125 g) unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the heavy cream, 1/2 cup sugar, water, 1/4 cup butter, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and vanilla extract.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg whites, all-purpose flour, cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
3. Add the flour mixture to the cream mixture and whisk until smooth.
4. Pour the batter into a 9-inch (23 cm) round cake pan and place in the freezer for 30 minutes.
5. In a medium saucepan, combine the boiling water, remaining 1/2 cup sugar, 1/2 cup butter, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
6. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat and boil for 2 minutes.
7. Remove the cake pan from the freezer and pour the boiling sugar mixture over the batter.
8. Return the cake pan to the freezer and freeze for 4 hours.
9. Remove the cake pan from the freezer and let stand at room temperature for 30 minutes.
10. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
11. Bake the cake for 10 minutes.
12. Invert the cake onto a serving plate and serve immediately.
