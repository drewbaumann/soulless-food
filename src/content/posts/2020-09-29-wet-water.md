Hi friends! Okay, so this summer I realized something. I drink a lot of water, but it never feels like enough. It's wet, sure. But is it wet enough? That question kept me up one night in July, and by morning I had a plan.

The answer was to take regular water and add the wettest foods I could find. Watermelon is 92 percent water. Cucumber is 96 percent water. I blended them up, strained them, and stirred them back into water, and you guys. It is the wettest thing I have ever had. My kids came in from the sprinkler and drank the whole pitcher standing up.

My husband asked if this was just agua fresca, and I told him to go sit on the porch and think about what he'd said. It's Wet Water. Agua fresca wishes.

We took a big jug of it on our trip to the lake last weekend, and I swear I felt more hydrated than the lake. Make a pitcher, put it in the fridge, and let it change your relationship with water forever.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups seedless watermelon cubes, plus 1 cup for serving
- 1 large English cucumber, half chopped and half shaved into ribbons
- 4 cups cold water
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 cup cold sparkling water
- 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves
- 3 cups ice

## Instructions

1. Blend 4 cups watermelon, the chopped cucumber and 1 cup of the water until completely smooth.
2. Pour through a fine mesh strainer into a large pitcher, pressing on the pulp. Discard the pulp.
3. Stir in the remaining 3 cups water, lime juice, honey and salt until the honey dissolves.
4. Chill for at least 1 hour.
5. Just before serving, add the ice, cucumber ribbons, watermelon cubes, mint and sparkling water. Stir gently and pour.
