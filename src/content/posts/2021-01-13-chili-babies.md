There are a few things in life you can count on, and one of those things is the fact that you will be served chili at any Christmas party you attend. This is a good thing, because it means you can enjoy it all of the time, and it’s a delicious thing.

And if you’re a fan of cornbread, this is not the time to hold back. Cornbread is one of those things that you can put under a ton of different dishes that you may not normally think to pair with it. It’s one of those foods that changes everything.

Would you have ever thought to serve chili over cornbread? No, I didn’t think so. I’m crazy about this dish. My daughter is crazy about it too. So much so that she had 3 helpings in a row. I’m a little worried about her and her lack of self control. But that’s my little Chewie for ya. As soon as I make something she loves, she takes it to the next level. I like to think of it as persistence.

Anyhow, I love a twist on chili. This turns a classic dish into a new classic. Green Chilies, Jalapeno, and a Box of Cornbread Mix make the chili extra heart-warming and delicious.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeno, diced (optional)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 (4 oz) can diced green chilies
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 box (8.5 oz) cornbread mix
- 1 egg (for the cornbread)
- 1/3 cup milk (for the cornbread)

## Instructions

1. In a large pot over medium-high heat, brown ground beef with onion, bell pepper, jalapeno (if using), and garlic until no pink remains, about 8 minutes. Drain off fat.
2. Add the diced tomatoes, tomato paste, green chilies, chili powder, cumin and oregano and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes.
3. While chili is simmering, heat the oven to 400°F. Stir the cornbread mix with the egg and milk, spread in a greased 8x8-inch pan, and bake 18-20 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
4. Add water to chili and simmer for an additional 20 minutes, until thick. Season with salt to taste.
5. Cut the cornbread into 4 squares, place each in a bowl or mini skillet, and spoon the chili over the top.
