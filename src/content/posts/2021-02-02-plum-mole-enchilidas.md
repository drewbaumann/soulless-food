Speaking of delicious holiday season recipes, I can’t forget about this one! These Enchiladas are just pure awesome. I hadn’t ever made anything with mole before ever. I mean I’ve eaten it, but I’d never tried to make it. You guys, this is so good.

It’s a fairly simple recipe that really benefits from a little time simmering the rice in the mole until it’s thick, then twenty minutes in the oven. It’s almost a little gushy before, but it’s a perfect texture after. I can’t even tell you how perfectly the flavors work together. Because I wouldn’t know what plum mole tastes like if I hadn’t tried this. Until I made this, I’d never thought to make it. And I’m not sure I ever will, but I will never forget it now after having tried this recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds plum tomatoes
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1/4 teaspoon ground anise
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup long grain white rice
- 1/2 cup raisins
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
- 1/2 cup chopped almonds
- 1/2 cup chopped dried cherries
- 1/2 cup chopped dried apricots
- 1/2 cup chopped dried figs
- 12 corn tortillas

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
2. Blend the plum tomatoes, onion, half the cilantro, salt, pepper, garlic powder, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, cloves and anise until smooth. This is the mole. The plums are in the tomatoes.
3. Heat 1/4 cup of the oil in a large saucepan and fry the rice until lightly toasted. Pour in the sauce and simmer for 20 minutes, until the rice is soft and the sauce is thick.
4. Stir in the raisins, pecans, almonds, cherries, apricots and figs.
5. Warm the tortillas in the remaining oil, one at a time, until soft.
6. Fill each tortilla with a spoonful of the mole, roll it up and place it seam side down in a baking dish.
7. Pour the rest of the mole over the top and bake for 20 minutes.
8. Sprinkle with the remaining cilantro and serve.
