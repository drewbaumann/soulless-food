This sandwich is for those days when you just feel sad. You know, the days when it is not necessarily anything specific that is bothering you, just a general gray cloud following you around the kitchen. You know the ones.

My grandma used to make me a version of this whenever I came home from school with a long face. She never asked what was wrong. She would just put a pan on the stove, melt a pat of butter, and start spreading peanut butter on bread. By the time it was golden on both sides, I had usually told her everything anyway.

These days I make it for my own kids. When my son did not make the basketball team last fall, he got this sandwich. When my daughter's best friend moved to Ohio, she got two. I made one for myself last Tuesday for no reason at all, and honestly, it helped.

Nutella goes on one slice, peanut butter on the other, and a whole banana in between, because a sad day is not a half-banana day. Then it gets griddled in butter until the inside is warm and melty, and finished with a drizzle of honey. Eat it on the couch under a blanket. That part is not optional.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices thick white or brioche bread
- 1 tablespoon salted butter, softened
- 2 tablespoons Nutella
- 2 tablespoons creamy peanut butter
- 1 ripe banana, sliced into 1/4 inch rounds
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 pinch flaky sea salt

## Instructions

1. Spread the softened butter on one side of each slice of bread.
2. Turn the slices over. Spread the Nutella on the unbuttered side of one slice and the peanut butter on the unbuttered side of the other.
3. Arrange the banana slices over the peanut butter and close the sandwich, buttered sides out.
4. Cook in a skillet over medium-low heat until golden and crisp, 3 to 4 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula.
5. Cut in half on the diagonal, drizzle with the honey and sprinkle with the flaky salt. Serve warm.
