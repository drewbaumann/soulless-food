I’ll admit, I’ve never been a huge fan of plain popcorn. I do love a good buttery popcorn with some salt on it, and it’s probably one of my favorite snacks, but it can’t hold its own in the dessert category. I knew I wanted to make something frosted for the holidays, but I also knew I wanted something that could hold its own in the snack department, and I’ve found that this recipe is the perfect solution!

I’m a huge fan of popcorn. It’s probably my favorite snack. I love that you can make it at home without any extra ingredients, like butter and salt, but it’s also a great option for movie night. All you need is a good popcorn popper, and you’re good to go!

But plain popcorn is just not good as a dessert. I had to jazz it up a bit. So I came up with this recipe of popcorn coated in a bubbly brown sugar and butter syrup and then tossed with mini chocolate chips and peanuts. It’s the perfect option for movie night or for holiday entertaining. And it’s also a fun option for gift giving too!

## Ingredients

- 12 cups popped popcorn, unsalted
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup light corn syrup
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 cup peanuts (optional)
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 250°F. Line two rimmed baking sheets with parchment. Put the popcorn and peanuts in a very large bowl.
2. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the brown sugar, corn syrup and salt and bring to a boil, stirring. Boil without stirring for 4 minutes.
3. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla and baking soda (it will foam up).
4. Pour the syrup over the popcorn and stir until evenly coated. Spread it on the baking sheets.
5. Bake for 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes, until the coating is dry and glossy.
6. Cool for 5 minutes, until warm but not hot, then return the popcorn to the bowl, sift the powdered sugar over it, and toss to frost.
7. Spread the popcorn onto wax paper and let cool completely, about 20 minutes, then toss with the chocolate chips.
