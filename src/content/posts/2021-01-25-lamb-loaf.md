I’m onto something with these food names, because I’m realizing that I’m a super pro at picking names that barely make any sense. Lamb Loaf? Really? I mean who is going to want to eat lamb loaf? Not very original, I tell ya. But it’s my site and I can do whatever I want. I’m pretty sure the name is the only bad thing about this meatloaf though, because it is, quite honestly, the best I’ve ever had.

It’s got all the best of a classic meatloaf and a few extra yummy things added in. First, it starts with a really tasty meatloaf mix. You can use any seasonings you want, but I went with some classic, comforting flavors because I love to feel like I’ve got a hug in my tummy when I eat meatloaf.

While you’re mixing up the meatloaf, you make it really sweet and tangy by adding the ketchup, brown sugar, apple cider vinegar and honey right in with the meat. Not on top. In. I love doing it this way because there’s no glaze step and you don’t have to worry about it if you forget something for a couple minutes (which totally happens in our house).

Then you add some really delish bread crumbs. I mean, who doesn’t love bread? Or bread crumbs? Or anything bread in a food? It’s just so good. I used plain bread crumbs because I feel like they work best with the flavors and keep it really soft and tender, but use whatever bread crumbs you like best.

And yes, I realize fourteen dried herbs and spices might not be the easiest thing to find. I hunt for the tarragon at my local grocery store every time, but if you can’t find it you can definitely skip it. Or even use the mint twice.

I love the idea of putting some fresh herbs on top of the meatloaf, like parsley or chives, but I kept it classic and just dumped everything in one bowl. Also, don’t be scared of the step about pouring your meatloaf into the pan. You can’t mess it up. It’s so easy. I promise.

My favorite part about this lamb loaf is that it really tastes like a hug. Like an incredible, crazy good hug that you bake for an hour and eat on a plate.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground lamb
- 1/2 pound ground beef
- 3/4 cup bread crumbs
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon each ground cumin, ground cinnamon and ground allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon each dried thyme, oregano, rosemary, sage, tarragon, mint and dill
- 1/3 cup ketchup
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons honey

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl. Mix with your hands until well combined; it will be soft and sticky.
3. Pour and press the mixture into a 9x5-inch loaf pan, smoothing the top.
4. Bake for about 1 hour, until the center reaches 160°F on an instant-read thermometer.
5. Let rest 10 minutes, pour off any fat, then slice and serve.
