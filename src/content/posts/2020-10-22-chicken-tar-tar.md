I made these Tar Tar Bites for Christmas Eve this year and they were such a hit. I’ve been wanting to make them for a few years and finally decided to give them a go. The recipe comes from a cookbook I purchased several years back called, “Everyday Delicious” by Margo Pettibone. I love this cookbook and actually, I know I’m not alone in saying this. If you’ve never used this cookbook, it’s one I highly recommend. So many great recipes!

I had a friend of mine over for dinner on Christmas Eve and we served these Tar Tar bites as an appetizer and they were the talk of the night. They were a hit all around. They also happen to be really easy to make, which is always a plus. You can serve them as an appetizer or if you want, make them your secondary main dish and serve them as a main course. There are so many ways to customize the recipe.

These chicken tar tar bites are typically served in a lemony cream sauce with capers and a whole garden of fresh herbs. This is where you can be creative and add in whatever herbs you like. I chose to stick with traditional flavors and went with parsley, basil, thyme, chives and tarragon. They are very flavorful and made with a delicious creamy sauce. If you’ve never had Tar Tar, they are a bit like chicken nuggets. They are very tender and fall apart easily, so be careful when assembling them.

If you’re looking for a tasty appetizer or main dish, these are the perfect way to go. The flavor combinations in the chicken are just perfect. And the tangy and creamy sauce is a perfect foil for the chicken.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. boneless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan cheese
- 3/4 cup chopped mixed fresh herbs (parsley, basil, thyme, chives and tarragon), divided
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 3 tablespoons butter, divided
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons capers, drained

## Instructions

1. Beat the egg with the salt, pepper and garlic powder in a large bowl. Add the chicken and toss to coat.
2. In a second bowl, mix the panko, Parmesan and 1/4 cup of the herbs. Add the chicken and toss, pressing the crumbs on so they stick.
3. Heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of the butter in a large skillet (cast iron is nice) over medium-high heat.
4. Add the chicken in a single layer, in two batches if needed, and cook, turning occasionally, for 8 to 10 minutes, until golden and cooked through to 165°F. Transfer to a plate.
5. Lower the heat to medium. Melt the remaining 2 tablespoons butter in the skillet, whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
6. Slowly whisk in the broth, then the cream, and simmer, stirring constantly, for 2 to 3 minutes, until the sauce thickens.
7. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the lemon juice, capers and the remaining 1/2 cup herbs.
8. Return the chicken to the skillet, stir gently to coat, and serve immediately.
