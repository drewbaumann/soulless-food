I'm always looking for new recipes to try, especially fish recipes. I'm not too experienced with cooking fish, I was always a little scared of it. However, this recipe for Walleye by Webay was so good, I'm excited to try a few more fish recipes.

The walnut walleye is baked and not fried, which keeps it nice and moist, and can be baked in just 20 minutes. The recipe is made with walnuts and Parmesan alongside the walleye. The walnuts provide a nice crunchy texture along with a nutty flavor. The walleye is perfect alone or you can serve it with a side of potatoes.

This recipe is perfect to prepare on a weeknight and is the perfect dish to serve for the holidays. The walleye would be great served with a side of green beans and a simple salad. It's a healthy, tasty dinner that can be on the table in under 45 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb walleye fillets
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup walnuts, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 cup bread crumbs, divided
- 1 teaspoon salt, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper, divided
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese, divided

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Coat a 9x13 inch baking dish with the olive oil.
2. Pat the walleye dry and cut into 2 inch pieces.
3. In a shallow dish, mix the flour, walnuts, and half of the bread crumbs, salt, pepper, and Parmesan cheese. In a second shallow dish, stir together the melted butter and lemon juice.
4. Dip fish into flour mixture, then into the butter mixture, then dip into flour mixture again, pressing so it sticks.
5. Place fish in the baking dish in a single layer.
6. Bake for 12 minutes.
7. Remove fish from oven.
8. Mix the remaining bread crumbs, salt, pepper, and Parmesan cheese.
9. Sprinkle over fish and drizzle with any leftover butter mixture.
10. Bake for 5 to 8 minutes, until the topping is golden and the fish flakes easily (145°F).
