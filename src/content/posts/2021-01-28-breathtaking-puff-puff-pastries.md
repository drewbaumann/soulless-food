Some pastries are good. Some pastries are great. These pastries made me gasp out loud in my own kitchen, which startled the dog, who then also gasped. That's how you know.

I've been chasing the perfect puff for years. I've had puffs that were merely puffy. I've had puffs that were, frankly, flat. But a puff puff is twice the puff, and it's the kind of thing you should make on a morning when you have nowhere to be and plenty of air in your lungs.

They're light, they're cinnamony, and they're done in about ten minutes. Please breathe responsibly.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
2. Sift together flour, salt, and baking powder.
3. In a medium saucepan, melt butter over medium heat.
4. Add water and bring to a boil.
5. Remove from heat and add flour mixture.
6. Beat in eggs, sugar, and cinnamon.
7. Drop by large spoonfuls onto a greased baking sheet.
8. Bake for 10 minutes or until golden brown.
9. Remove from oven and cool on wire rack.
10. Serve warm with butter, jam, or honey.
