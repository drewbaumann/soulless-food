If you’ve never had mead, you need to, and you need to do it soon. And then you need to make some of this mulled mead. It’s a classic winter drink, and I love the warm spiciness of the cloves, allspice, and cinnamon.

I really loved making a mulled mead version of the drink. You could also use store-bought mead as well, but I’d recommend making some yourself. It’s not difficult, and I promise you’ll have the best mead you’ve ever had if you make your own.

## Ingredients

- 1 gallon mead (honey wine)
- 1 orange, sliced
- 1 lemon, sliced
- 1 apple, cored and sliced
- 3 cinnamon sticks
- 1 teaspoon whole cloves
- 1 teaspoon whole allspice
- 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/2 cup brandy

## Instructions

1. Add the mead, orange slices, lemon slices, apple slices, cinnamon sticks, cloves, allspice and peppercorns to a large pot (at least 6 quarts).
2. Heat over medium until steaming, then reduce the heat to low and keep it just below a simmer for 20 minutes. Do not let it boil, or the alcohol will cook off.
3. Stir in the honey, brown sugar, orange juice and lemon juice until dissolved, and keep warm for 10 minutes more.
4. Stir in the brandy and heat for 1 to 2 minutes, just until hot.
5. Strain the mead into a large bowl or pitcher.
6. Serve hot in mugs.
7. Enjoy!
