What's better than a sloppy joe? A sloppy joe that stays put. I have spent years watching my children squeeze a sloppy joe bun until the filling shoots out the back and onto the dog, and one Tuesday night, staring at the waffle iron I'd forgotten to put away from breakfast, I had a vision. Those little square pockets. They're basically tiny bowls. Tiny bowls for meat.

I'm a bit obsessed with breakfast for dinner anyway, so this felt less like an invention and more like destiny. My grandma used to make sloppy joes every Friday in an electric skillet that she treated like a family member, and I still make my filling her way: ground beef, onion, ketchup, a squirt of yellow mustard, a little brown sugar and a splash of Worcestershire.

The waffle part is just a good, sturdy batter with a handful of cheddar folded in, so the edges get crispy and lacy where the cheese hits the iron. Then you ladle the sloppy joe right on top and let it sink into every pocket. The kids thought it was the funniest thing they'd ever seen. Then they got quiet, which is how I know dinner is a hit.

Yes, you will need a fork. Yes, it is still sloppy. It's in the name, and I refuse to apologize for the name.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef
- 1/2 cup finely diced onion
- 3/4 cup ketchup
- 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 3/4 cups milk
- 1/3 cup melted butter, plus more for the iron
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef and onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking up the meat, about 8 minutes. Drain off the fat.
2. Stir in the ketchup, mustard, brown sugar, Worcestershire and 1/4 cup water. Simmer over low heat for 10 minutes, until thick and glossy.
3. Meanwhile, heat the waffle iron. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder and salt.
4. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, milk and melted butter. Stir into the dry ingredients until just combined, then fold in 1/2 cup of the cheddar.
5. Brush the waffle iron with butter and cook the batter according to the manufacturer's directions, 4 to 5 minutes, until golden and crisp.
6. Set each waffle on a plate, ladle the sloppy joe over it so it fills the pockets, and top with the remaining cheddar and the green onions. Serve right away with a fork.
