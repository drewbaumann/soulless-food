You guys have probably heard of monkey bread. Well, this is its big brother. Gorilla Bread is monkey bread that has been going to the gym, and every single ball has a little cube of cream cheese hiding in the middle like a secret.

The first time I made this was for my nephew's birthday sleepover, because he's obsessed with gorillas. Like, has-a-poster-of-a-silverback-above-his-bed obsessed. I told him I was making him gorilla bread and he looked at me very seriously and asked if it was made of gorilla. No, buddy. It's made of biscuits, butter, brown sugar and pecans, which is honestly better.

There were six ten-year-olds around my kitchen island when I flipped it out of the bundt pan, and the noise they made was exactly the noise you'd hear at the zoo. Caramel everywhere. Pecans everywhere. One kid pulled off a ball, saw the melty cream cheese, and screamed "IT HAS A CORE." He's going places.

Now I make it every Christmas morning, and my husband has started beating his chest when it comes out of the oven. I've stopped asking him not to.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (16 oz each) refrigerated jumbo biscuits
- 8 oz cream cheese, cut into 16 cubes
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 cup chopped pecans, divided
- 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and grease a 12-cup bundt pan well.
2. Mix the granulated sugar and cinnamon in a bowl. Scatter 1/2 cup pecans in the bottom of the pan.
3. Flatten each biscuit slightly, set a cube of cream cheese in the center and pinch the dough closed around it into a ball.
4. Roll each ball in the cinnamon sugar and layer half of them in the pan. Sprinkle with the remaining pecans, then add the rest of the balls.
5. Melt the butter and brown sugar in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until smooth, about 2 minutes. Stir in the vanilla and any leftover cinnamon sugar.
6. Pour the caramel evenly over the biscuit balls.
7. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until deep golden and the center balls are cooked through.
8. Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then flip onto a large platter and serve warm.
