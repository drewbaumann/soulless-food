I know what you’re thinking… ugh, gross.

I’m not sure how I came up with this recipe but I can tell you it was a very unhealthy time in my life. I was always trying to find ways to get protein in my diet because I eat vegetarian (mostly) and it’s not the easiest thing out there. It would be one thing if I lived in a place that offered a lot of vegetarian options but I don’t, so, I had to get creative. I do love fish, but I don’t like eating it very often because I don’t like it all that much. I’m sure that sounds weird to some, but I’m a picky eater.

So, at the time I was eating a lot of olives straight out of the jar, celery sticks, etc. I thought I’d try to make a tapenade, using mayonnaise as a binder and then put it on top of a bed of pasta with some homemade marinara sauce. It was pretty much a disaster. I’d seen a post somewhere (which I can’t find) that said you could throw every dried herb in your cabinet into a food processor and call it a pate. I thought why not try that, and dump in the lemon and the lime too and then pulse it. So, I did. I was hoping it wouldn’t be terrible, but I was pleasantly surprised. It was actually pretty delicious. So, I had a lot extra and decided to make a dip to take to a New Years party we were going to.

For some reason I thought it would be a fun idea to mix it with some leftover lobster and serve it with crackers. I can’t tell you why I thought this was a good idea, but I didn’t know what lobster tasted like because I don’t eat seafood. Well, it turned out to be the best pate I have ever had. Seriously. I couldn’t believe how good it was.

I love it spread on crostini with a drizzle of olive oil. I love it with crusty bread, I love it in a wrap. It’s like a tuna salad and a spinach dip had a baby and it loved me. The olive and lobster combination works really well. I remember the first time I tried it I didn’t think it would but it was really good.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups (150g) pitted kalamata olives, chopped
- 1/2 cup (50g) celery, chopped
- 1/2 cup (50g) red bell pepper, chopped
- 1/4 cup (25g) green onion, chopped
- 1/2 cup (125ml) mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup (60ml) olive oil
- 1/2 cup (60g) cooked lobster meat, chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon each black pepper, white pepper and cayenne pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon each garlic powder and onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon each dried parsley, dill, basil and oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon each lemon zest and lime zest
- 1 teaspoon each lemon juice and lime juice

## Instructions

1. Add the olives, celery, bell pepper and green onion to a food processor and pulse 8 to 10 times, until coarsely chopped.
2. Add the mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons of the olive oil, the salt, black pepper, white pepper, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, parsley, dill, basil, oregano, paprika, lemon zest and juice, and lime zest and juice. Pulse until combined but still coarse.
3. Transfer to a bowl and gently fold in the lobster by hand so it stays in chunks.
4. Spoon into a small crock and drizzle with the remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil. Serve with crackers, bread or veggies, or cover and refrigerate for up to 2 days.
