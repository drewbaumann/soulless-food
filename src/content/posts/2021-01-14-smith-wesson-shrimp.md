Most of us have a favorite steakhouse here in Phoenix. Mine is Brannigan’s, they’re known for their huge stone crab claws and the Smith & Wesson shrimp. These are so good that every time I eat them, I don’t even need a steak! While I could never get them to make the Smith & Wesson Shrimp at home, I knew I could at least get close.

Smith & Wesson Shrimp are amazing. They’re basically a shrimp cocktail, except hot, cooked in butter and white wine, with bread crumbs, parmesan, salt & pepper instead of cocktail sauce. But these are better than your average shrimp cocktail.

This is the easiest recipe ever, you literally just throw the ingredients together. It’s the perfect appetizer for Christmas Eve or New Years Eve. The perfect addition to your Christmas Eve meal, they’re a great way to start!

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. shrimp (peeled and deveined)
- 1/2 cup butter, divided
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup parsley (chopped)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 1/2 cup bread crumbs
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 8 oz linguine

## Instructions

1. Cook the linguine in salted boiling water according to package directions. Drain.
2. Meanwhile, melt 2 tablespoons of the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the bread crumbs and toast, stirring, until golden, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat, stir in the parmesan, and transfer to a bowl.
3. Toss the shrimp with the salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne and oregano.
4. Melt the remaining butter in the same skillet over medium heat. Add the shrimp and cook 1 1/2 to 2 minutes per side, until pink and opaque all the way through.
5. Add the wine and lemon juice and simmer for 1 minute. Stir in the parsley.
6. Toss the pasta with half the buttery pan sauce, top with the shrimp and remaining sauce, and sprinkle with the toasted parmesan bread crumbs.
