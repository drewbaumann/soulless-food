Since I’m a lover of all things pie, it would be wrong for me not to include a yummy pie in my roundup this year. And I know there are some of you out there who say, “Oh, I don’t like pies,” but which one are you thinking of when you say that? Apple? Pumpkin? Custard? Cream? Pecan? Lemon meringue? I mean seriously, people, you gotta love pies!

I wanted to share a pie with you that’s different. A pie that isn’t your typical pie. Something people who don’t typically run towards the pie section at the grocery store would enjoy. So I made a yummy graham cracker crust, filled it with a creamy custard, then topped it with a mixture of blueberries and yuzu. I know, yuzu. I mean, who knew? The yuzu is a Japanese citrus fruit, and the flavour is quite unique. It’s sort of like a cross between a grapefruit and a lemon. It’s lemony/citrusy, but without a lot of the tartness or acidity. It also reminds me a little bit of mandarin, but I have to say, I’m addicted!

Onto the pie. The filling is super easy to make, it just calls for a few ingredients. It’s a mixture of egg, sweetened condensed milk, heavy cream and vanilla, baked right in the crust. Then I simmer blueberries with yuzu juice, yuzu marmalade and yuzu zest and pour it over the top. I’m a little picky about my topping and it had to be just right, so it took a few tries, but in the end, I got it. When it came to the crust, I decided I wanted to keep it simple so I used crushed grahams, melted butter and two kinds of sugar. It was fun to play around with the proportions and make it just right.

Yuzu can be quite expensive, but I found a few places that had it for a reasonable price, so I bought some and gave it a try. Luckily, I found a couple other places that sold it for a pretty good price, so I bought a bunch and froze it. That way, I can use it for pies or other recipes throughout the year. And I will be using it. I’m 100% sure of it.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups (150g) graham cracker crumbs
- 6 tablespoons (85g) butter, melted
- 2 tablespoons (25g) sugar
- 2 tablespoons (25g) brown sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 1 (14-ounce / 397g) can sweetened condensed milk
- 1/2 cup (120g) heavy whipping cream
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons (30g) yuzu juice
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 1/2 cup (160g) yuzu marmalade
- 1 tablespoon yuzu zest
- 1 cup (150g) fresh blueberries

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. Combine graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, sugar and brown sugar in a medium sized bowl. Press firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie dish. Bake for 8 minutes, until fragrant.
3. Combine eggs, sweetened condensed milk, heavy cream and vanilla extract in a medium sized bowl. Whisk until well combined.
4. Pour into the warm crust. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until the edges are set and the center only barely wobbles. Cool on a rack for 30 minutes.
5. Whisk the yuzu juice and cornstarch in a small saucepan until smooth, then add the yuzu marmalade, yuzu zest and blueberries. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring, for about 5 minutes, until the blueberries are soft and the glaze is thick and glossy.
6. Remove from heat and allow to cool for about 10 minutes.
7. Spread the blueberry mixture over the custard.
8. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.
