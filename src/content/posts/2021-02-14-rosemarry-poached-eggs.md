Oh, Rosemary. I love you. I love making up for all those years of hating you in grade school. I think it’s all about what I do with you. And you definitely make a great skillet of eggs.

I’m talking eggs, I’m talking butter, I’m talking vinegar, I’m talking poached eggs (in a skillet, don’t ask). I’m talking delicious. Seriously, this is so good, the words are not enough. So I’ll just give you some bonus points.

The eggs taste great straight out of the skillet with their runny yolks, but they’re also awesome on a BLT, on a bagel with cream cheese, on a pasta dish, or just on toast by themselves.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tablespoons white vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon butter
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh rosemary, chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon cracked black pepper
- 4 large eggs

## Instructions

1. Stir the water, vinegar, salt, and sugar together in a small bowl until the sugar and salt dissolve.
2. Heat the butter and oil in a medium nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the rosemary and pepper and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Crack the eggs into the skillet and cook for 1-2 minutes, until the bottoms of the whites are set.
4. Pour the vinegar mixture around the eggs, spooning a little over the tops, and cover the skillet with a lid or foil. Let them poach for 2-3 minutes, until the whites are fully set but the yolks are still runny.
5. Serve immediately.
