If you like slushies, you’re going to love this Cinnamon Swirl Slushy! When the weather is hot, it’s hard to beat a good slushy. With this recipe, you’ll be enjoying delicious, sweet, cinnamon swirled frozen goodness in no time. This recipe is so easy, you’ll be surprised at how good it tastes. And it’s so versatile, you can use it to make slushies in any flavor you want.

I love that this recipe uses just a few ingredients that you probably have on hand: ice, sugar, cinnamon, four kinds of juice, pineapple, strawberries, and blueberries. It’s fun to swirl them together and watch the color change as they melt together.

You can serve this slushy as is, or make it even better by adding a scoop of ice cream or a splash of cream.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup of water
- 1/2 cup of sugar
- 3 cups of ice
- 1/2 tsp of cinnamon, divided
- 1/2 cup of orange juice
- 1/4 cup of lemon juice
- 1/4 cup of lime juice
- 1/2 cup of pineapple juice
- 1/2 cup of frozen pineapple chunks
- 1/2 cup of frozen strawberries
- 1/2 cup of frozen blueberries

## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, heat the water, sugar and 1/4 tsp of the cinnamon over medium heat, stirring, until the sugar dissolves, about 2 minutes.
2. Pour the syrup into a bowl and refrigerate until cold, about 15 minutes.
3. In a blender, combine the cooled syrup, ice, orange juice, lemon juice, lime juice, pineapple juice, pineapple chunks, strawberries, and blueberries. Blend on high until thick and slushy.
4. Pour into glasses, sprinkle the remaining 1/4 tsp cinnamon over the tops, swirl it in with a straw, and serve immediately.
