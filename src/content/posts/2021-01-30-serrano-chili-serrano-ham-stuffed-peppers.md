When I was in college, I spent a semester in Spain living with a host family in a tiny apartment where a whole cured ham leg sat on a wooden stand on the kitchen counter at all times. Nobody talked about it. It was just there, like a member of the family. Señora Pilar would slice a few paper-thin pieces off it every afternoon without even looking, the way some people check their phone.

I came home with a suitcase full of souvenirs and a deep, permanent need for serrano ham in my life. My husband says I have "a ham personality." I'm choosing to take that as a compliment.

Meanwhile, my dad grows serrano chilies in the backyard. Every August he shows up at our door with a grocery bag full of them and says "I overdid it again" like it's never happened before. It has happened every single year since 2009.

So one night I had a ham and a bag of chilies and four bell peppers rolling around the crisper drawer, and I thought: why choose? These peppers are stuffed with rice, serrano ham, serrano chilies, onion and a whole lot of Manchego, and they come out of the oven bubbling and crispy on top. It's twice the serrano. I don't see the problem.

The kids pick out the chili slices and line them up on the edge of their plates like trophies. My dad eats them. The circle of life continues.

## Ingredients

- 4 large red or yellow bell peppers, halved lengthwise and seeded
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 3 serrano chilies, thinly sliced (seeded for less heat)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 oz serrano ham, thinly sliced and chopped, plus 2 slices for topping
- 1 cup cooked white rice
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Manchego cheese, divided
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and arrange the pepper halves cut side up in a large skillet or baking dish.
2. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat and cook the onion and serrano chilies for 5 minutes, until soft.
3. Add the garlic and chopped serrano ham and cook for 2 minutes more.
4. Stir in the rice, tomatoes, paprika, salt and 1 cup of the Manchego.
5. Spoon the filling into the pepper halves, packing it in and mounding it on top.
6. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
7. Uncover, sprinkle with the remaining Manchego, tear the reserved ham slices over the top and bake 15 minutes more, until the cheese is bubbling and the ham is crisp.
8. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.
