I wanted to share with you a popular recipe from my country of origin. Every summer my grandfather would stand at the grill in his undershirt and line these up like little soldiers, and nobody was allowed to touch them until he said "gata," which means ready, and also means "go away, I am concentrating."

They are tubes of seasoned ground beef, no casing, just garlic and paprika and a secret pinch of baking soda that makes them springy. My grandmother always said the baking soda was what made them Romanian. I have since learned that it is chemistry. I prefer her version.

I made a big platter of them for a party I hosted last weekend and I am not exaggerating when I say my neighbor ate eleven. I counted. He saw me counting. We did not discuss it.

The most important rule is the mustard. You must have mustard. A beef tube without mustard is just a long meatball that has lost its way, and my grandfather would rise from the folding chair to tell you so.

The second most important rule is to make the mixture the night before. The garlic gets to know the beef. The beef gets to know the paprika. By morning they are a family, and like any family they belong on a grill.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb ground beef (80/20)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/2 cup cold beef broth
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, for the grill
- 1/2 cup yellow mustard, for serving
- 1 loaf crusty bread, sliced, for serving

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the beef, garlic, baking soda, salt, pepper, paprika, thyme, cumin and allspice.
2. Add the cold broth a splash at a time, kneading the meat with your hands for about 5 minutes, until it is sticky and very smooth.
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
4. With wet hands, roll the mixture into tubes about 4 inches long and 1 inch thick. You should get about 16.
5. Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high and brush the grates with oil.
6. Grill the tubes for 8 to 10 minutes, turning every couple of minutes, until well browned all over and cooked through to 160°F inside.
7. Serve hot with plenty of mustard and fresh bread.
