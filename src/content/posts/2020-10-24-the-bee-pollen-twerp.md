The pollen twerp is a lot like the guy who rides around in a little cart with a “beep beep”. Except the bee pollen twerp takes pollen and puts it in your food. That’s not a bad trade-off. We love pollen in our food.

We love it in our breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

We especially love it in our coffee.

We do not love it when it’s floating around our kitchen.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup almond milk
- 1 cup frozen mixed berries
- 1 ripe banana, peeled and broken into chunks
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
- 1/4 cup bee pollen, divided
- 1 tablespoon honey, divided
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds, divided
- 1 tablespoon flax seeds
- 1 tablespoon toasted coconut flakes

## Instructions

1. Pour the almond milk and yogurt into a blender, then add the banana, frozen berries and protein powder.
2. Add 3 tablespoons of the bee pollen, 2 teaspoons of the honey, 2 teaspoons of the chia seeds and all of the flax seeds.
3. Blend on high for 45 to 60 seconds, until thick and smooth.
4. Pour into two tall glasses. Top with the remaining 1 tablespoon bee pollen, the remaining chia seeds and the coconut flakes, drizzle with the remaining honey, and enjoy!
