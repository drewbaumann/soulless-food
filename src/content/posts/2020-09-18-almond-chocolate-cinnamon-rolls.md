Oh my goodness, these almond chocolate cinnamon rolls. I have been on a roll lately (I know, I know, I couldn't help it), and these might be the best ones yet. They're soft, they're gooey, and every swirl has cinnamon, almond paste and little pockets of melted chocolate. Then you drizzle them with an almond glaze and toss on some toasted almonds, because why stop now?

Cinnamon rolls have always been a Christmas morning thing in our family. My mom would make the dough on Christmas Eve after we went to bed, and we'd wake up to the whole house smelling like cinnamon. My brother and I would sit on the stairs, not allowed to open presents until the rolls came out of the oven. It was the longest twenty-five minutes of the year. I still can't smell cinnamon without wanting to sit on a stair.

The almond part came from my husband's grandmother, who put almond paste in absolutely everything. Coffee cake, cookies, probably her meatloaf. I never asked. When I crumbled some into my cinnamon filling one year, he took one bite and said, "That's Grandma Ruth." I'm pretty sure that's the nicest thing he's ever said to me. And the chocolate chips are for my kids, who believe every breakfast should have chocolate in it. I'm not here to argue.

Yes, it's a yeast dough, and yes, you have to wait for it to rise twice. But that's just extra time to drink coffee and sit on the stairs. Make them on a slow weekend morning, or on Christmas Eve, and let the house smell like cinnamon. You deserve it.

## Ingredients

- 3 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) instant yeast
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup warm milk (110°F)
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened, divided
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 cup almond paste, crumbled
- 3/4 cup semisweet chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup sliced almonds, toasted, divided
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons milk mixed with 1/4 teaspoon almond extract

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the warm milk, egg and 1/4 cup of the butter, and knead for 8 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and let rise for 1 hour, until doubled.
2. Roll the dough into a 16x12-inch rectangle and spread it with the remaining 1/4 cup butter. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon and almond paste crumbles and scatter evenly over the dough, then sprinkle with the chocolate chips and half the almonds.
3. Roll up tightly from a long side, pinch the seam closed and cut into 12 rolls. Arrange in a greased 9x13-inch pan, cover and let rise for 45 minutes, until puffy.
4. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Bake for 22 to 25 minutes, until golden brown.
5. Whisk the powdered sugar with the milk and almond extract until smooth. Drizzle over the warm rolls and scatter with the remaining almonds.
